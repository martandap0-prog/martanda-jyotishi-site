The Navāṃśa, or D9 chart, is one of the sixteen classical divisional charts (ṣoḍaśavarga)
described in *Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra*. While the Rāśi chart shows the raw material of
a life, the Navāṃśa reveals how that material has been refined — the strength a planet
truly carries once its promise is tested.

> "Rāśi shows the tree; Navāṃśa shows the fruit."
