Every journey through the zodiac begins with a spark. That beginning is **Meṣa**, the Ram, the first of the twelve rāśis.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Nature** | Masculine, odd, movable (*cara*) |
| **Element** | Fire (*agni*) |
| **Direction** | East (*prācī*) |
| **Varṇa** | Kṣatriya |
| **Rising** | Back-first (*pṛṣṭhodaya*), strong at night |
| **Lord** | Maṅgala (Mars), mūlatrikoṇa 0°–12° |
| **Exaltation** | Sun, deepest at 10° |
| **Debilitation** | Śani (Saturn), deepest at 20° |
| **Nakṣatras** | Aśvinī, Bharaṇī, Kṛttikā (first pāda) |
| **Kālapuruṣa** | The head (*śiras*) |

## The lesson of Meṣa

Fire moves quickly, sometimes too quickly. Fire that burns without direction becomes destruction. Fire that learns patience becomes a lamp, guiding others through the dark.

*Source for the sign's qualities: Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra, chapter on the nature of the rāśis.*
