Kalyāṇa Varmā's **Sārāvalī** gives one of the most detailed classical treatments of the Moon. In chapter 23, *Candracāra*, it takes the Moon through all twelve rāśis and, in each, describes what happens when the Sun, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus or Saturn aspects it. This article presents every one of those verses with the Sanskrit, a transliteration, the key words, the meaning, and the reasoning that connects each result to the planets and signs involved.

## About this chapter

Sārāvalī chapter 23, *Candracāra* ("the Moon's movement"), gives the effects of the Moon aspected by each planet in each of the twelve rāśis. The material is 72 short ślokas plus one closing rule (śloka 86).

The chapter repeats one pattern for every rāśi:

1. One long verse on the Moon placed in that rāśi, with no aspect.
2. Six ślokas, in *āryā* metre, on the Moon there when aspected by each planet, always in the same order: Sūrya, Maṅgala, Budha, Guru, Śukra, Śani.

The chapter treats only the seven grahas. Rāhu and Ketu do not cast aspects here, and the Moon cannot aspect itself.

**How each entry is set out:** the Devanāgarī text, the IAST transliteration, the key words, and then a plain-English meaning with an explanation of the astrological logic behind the result.

**Source and method:** the Sanskrit follows R. Santhanam's edition of Sārāvalī (Ranjan Publications, vol. 1, pp. 250–287), checked verse by verse against the printed page. Where the print is unclear, the reading is marked [?]. The translations and explanations here are original readings of the Sanskrit, not taken from any published English translation.

## 1. Moon in Meṣa (ślokas 2–7)

In Meṣa, the Moon sits in Maṅgala's fiery sign. Fiery aspects (Sun, Mars) give rulership with harshness. The benefics (Mercury, Jupiter, Venus) turn that energy into learning, office and comfort. Saturn's aspect is the only fully adverse one.

### Śloka 2 · Aspected by Sūrya

> अत्युग्रतरो नृपतिः प्रणतानां मार्दवं भजति जातः । धीरः सङ्ग्रामरुची रविणा दृष्टे शशिनि मेषे ॥२॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.2)*
>
> *atyugrataro nṛpatiḥ praṇatānāṃ mārdavaṃ bhajati jātaḥ | dhīraḥ saṅgrāmarucī raviṇā dṛṣṭe śaśini meṣe || 2 ||*

**Key words:** *atyugratara* very fierce · *nṛpati* king · *praṇata* one who bows · *mārdava* gentleness · *saṅgrāmaruci* fond of battle

**Meaning:** The native is a very fierce ruler, yet gentle with those who submit to him. He is steady and fond of battle.

**Explanation:** The Sun is exalted in Meṣa, so its aspect lends real authority (*nṛpati*) and a hard, martial edge. The Moon softens this only toward those who submit: the softness is conditional, which is the mark of a stern ruler rather than a cruel one.

### Śloka 3 · Aspected by Maṅgala

> दन्ताक्षिरोगतप्तः शिखिवातादिक्षतशरीरः । माण्डलिकः स्यान्मेषे कुजदृष्टे शशिनि भूतार्तः ॥३॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.3)*
>
> *dantākṣirogataptaḥ śikhivātādikṣataśarīraḥ | māṇḍalikaḥ syānmeṣe kujadṛṣṭe śaśini bhūtārtaḥ || 3 ||*

**Key words:** *danta* teeth · *akṣi* eyes · *śikhi* fire · *vāta* wind · *kṣata* wounded · *māṇḍalika* ruler of a district · *bhūtārta* troubled by spirits (or by creatures)

**Meaning:** He suffers from diseases of the teeth and eyes, and his body is hurt by fire, wind and the like. He becomes a regional ruler, but is troubled by spirits.

**Explanation:** Maṅgala aspects its own sign, so it gives a local lordship (*māṇḍalika*, smaller than the Sun's *nṛpati*). Mars as fire and the weapon shows up as burns, wounds and inflammation of the face (teeth, eyes). *Bhūtārta* reflects the Moon (mind) disturbed by a fierce graha.

### Śloka 4 · Aspected by Budha

> नानाविद्याचार्यः सद्वाक्यः स्यान्मनोऽभीष्टः । बुधदृष्टे मेषस्थे निशाकरे सत्कविर्विपुलकीर्तिः ॥४॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.4)*
>
> *nānāvidyācāryaḥ sadvākyaḥ syānmano'bhīṣṭaḥ | budhadṛṣṭe meṣasthe niśākare satkavirvipulakīrtiḥ || 4 ||*

**Key words:** *nānāvidyācārya* teacher of many sciences · *sadvākya* of good speech · *mano'bhīṣṭa* dear to the heart · *satkavi* a fine poet · *vipulakīrti* of wide fame

**Meaning:** He becomes a teacher of many branches of learning, speaks well, and is liked by others. He is a fine poet with wide fame.

**Explanation:** Budha rules learning and speech. Falling on the Moon in a quick, pioneering sign, its aspect makes the mind versatile and articulate, so learning becomes teaching and expression becomes poetry.

### Śloka 5 · Aspected by Guru

> बहुभृत्यधनसमृद्धो नृपतेः सचिवश्चमूपतिर्वाऽपि । मेषगृहे हिमरश्मौ दृष्टे गुरुणा पुमान् जातः ॥५॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.5)*
>
> *bahubhṛtyadhanasamṛddho nṛpateḥ sacivaścamūpatirvā'pi | meṣagṛhe himaraśmau dṛṣṭe guruṇā pumān jātaḥ || 5 ||*

**Key words:** *bahubhṛtya* many servants · *dhanasamṛddha* rich · *saciva* minister · *camūpati* commander of an army · *himaraśmi* the cool-rayed (Moon)

**Meaning:** He is rich in servants and wealth, and becomes a king's minister or the commander of his army.

**Explanation:** Guru is Maṅgala's friend and the adviser among the grahas. Its aspect does not make the native king himself (as the Sun did) but the trusted counsellor or general who serves the throne: authority by wisdom and trust.

### Śloka 6 · Aspected by Śukra

> सुभगः सुतधनयुक्तो वरयुवतिविभूषणोऽल्पभोक्ता च । मेषे शिशिरमयूखे भृगुतनयनिरीक्षिते भवति ॥६॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.6)*
>
> *subhagaḥ sutadhanayukto varayuvativibhūṣaṇo'lpabhoktā ca | meṣe śiśiramayūkhe bhṛgutanayanirīkṣite bhavati || 6 ||*

**Key words:** *subhaga* charming, fortunate · *suta* sons · *varayuvati* an excellent young woman · *vibhūṣaṇa* adorned by · *alpabhoktā* eats little · *bhṛgutanaya* son of Bhṛgu (Śukra)

**Meaning:** He is charming and fortunate, has sons and wealth, is graced by a fine young wife, and eats sparingly.

**Explanation:** Śukra brings attractiveness and a good spouse. *Alpabhoktā* (light eater) is a refinement: Venus in an Aries context gives taste rather than appetite.

### Śloka 7 · Aspected by Śani

> विद्विष्टो बहुदुःखी दारिद्र्यतनुर्मलीमसोऽनृतवाक् । मेषे शिशिरमयूखे रवितनयनिरीक्षिते भवति ॥७॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.7)*
>
> *vidviṣṭo bahuduḥkhī dāridryatanurmalīmaso'nṛtavāk | meṣe śiśiramayūkhe ravitanayanirīkṣite bhavati || 7 ||*

**Key words:** *vidviṣṭa* disliked · *bahuduḥkhī* very sorrowful · *dāridrya* poverty · *tanu* body (here: wasted, lean) · *malīmasa* unclean · *anṛtavāk* untruthful · *ravitanaya* son of the Sun (Śani)

**Meaning:** He is disliked, full of sorrow, wasted by poverty, unclean, and untruthful.

**Explanation:** Śani is debilitated in Meṣa, and its aspect on the Moon there brings Saturn's deprivation into the mind and the circumstances: lack, neglect of the body, and speech bent by need. Note that the debilitation sign of the aspecting graha matters, as śloka 86 confirms.

## 2. Moon in Vṛṣabha (ślokas 9–15)

Vṛṣabha is the Moon's exaltation sign and Śukra's earthy sign, so most aspects here turn into wealth, land and comfort. Mars hurts the marriage, and Saturn hurts money and the mother. Even Saturn leaves the native with sons, friends and kin.

### Śloka 9 · Aspected by Sūrya

> कर्षकमतिकर्मकरं द्विपदचतुष्पदसमृद्धमत्याढ्यम् । प्रायोगिकं प्रकुरुते वृषभे रविवीक्षितश्चन्द्रः ॥९॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.9)*
>
> *karṣakamatikarmakaraṃ dvipadacatuṣpadasamṛddhamatyāḍhyam | prāyogikaṃ prakurute vṛṣabhe ravivīkṣitaścandraḥ || 9 ||*

**Key words:** *karṣaka* cultivator · *atikarmakara* a great worker · *dvipada-catuṣpada* two-footed and four-footed (servants and cattle) · *atyāḍhya* very rich · *prāyogika* practical, skilled in applying things

**Meaning:** He becomes a cultivator and a hard worker, rich in servants and cattle, very wealthy, and practical in his dealings.

**Explanation:** Vṛṣabha is the earth and the herd. The Sun's aspect makes the native work that land with effort and authority, so the wealth comes as fields, livestock and the people who tend them.

### Śloka 10 · Aspected by Maṅgala

> अतिकामं कुजदृष्टो युवतिकृते नष्टदारमित्रजनम् । हृदयहरं नारीणां मातुर्न शुभं शशी वृषे कुरुते ॥१०॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.10)*
>
> *atikāmaṃ kujadṛṣṭo yuvatikṛte naṣṭadāramitrajanam | hṛdayaharaṃ nārīṇāṃ māturna śubhaṃ śaśī vṛṣe kurute || 10 ||*

**Key words:** *atikāma* very passionate · *yuvatikṛte* on account of women · *naṣṭa* lost · *dāra* wife · *hṛdayahara* heart-stealing · *mātuḥ na śubham* not good for the mother

**Meaning:** He is very passionate, and because of women he loses his wife and his friends. He wins the hearts of women, but is not good for his mother.

**Explanation:** Mars aspecting the Moon in a Venus sign inflames desire (*kāma*). The same charm that attracts women breaks the marriage and old friendships. Mars on the Moon (the mother) adds friction with or suffering for the mother.

### Śloka 11 · Aspected by Budha

> प्राज्ञं वाक्यविधिज्ञं प्रमुदितमिष्टं समस्तभूतानाम् । जनयति बुधेन दृष्टः शशी वृषेऽनुपमगुणैर्युक्तम् ॥११॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.11)*
>
> *prājñaṃ vākyavidhijñaṃ pramuditamiṣṭaṃ samastabhūtānām | janayati budhena dṛṣṭaḥ śaśī vṛṣe'nupamaguṇairyuktam || 11 ||*

**Key words:** *prājña* wise · *vākyavidhijña* knowing the rules of speech · *pramudita* cheerful · *iṣṭa* dear · *samastabhūtānām* to all beings · *anupamaguṇa* matchless qualities

**Meaning:** The native is wise, a master of correct and effective speech, cheerful, dear to all beings, and endowed with matchless qualities.

**Explanation:** Budha is a friend of Śukra and natural ruler of speech. Its aspect on the exalted Moon gives a refined, contented mind that expresses itself well: Vṛṣabha is also the natural 2nd house of speech.

### Śloka 12 · Aspected by Guru

> स्थिरपुत्रदारसुहृदं मातापितृभक्तिमन्तमतिनिपुणम् । धार्मिकमतिविख्यातं गवि गुरुदृष्टः शशी कुरुते ॥१२॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.12)*
>
> *sthiraputradārasuhṛdaṃ mātāpitṛbhaktimantamatinipuṇam | dhārmikamativikhyātaṃ gavi gurudṛṣṭaḥ śaśī kurute || 12 ||*

**Key words:** *sthira* lasting · *putra-dāra-suhṛd* sons, wife, friends · *mātāpitṛbhaktimant* devoted to parents · *atinipuṇa* very skilful · *dhārmika* righteous · *gavi* in the Bull

**Meaning:** His sons, wife and friends stay with him for life. He is devoted to his parents, very capable, righteous and very famous.

**Explanation:** Vṛṣabha is a fixed sign, and Guru's aspect makes everything the Moon rules stable: family, friendships and loyalty to parents. Guru adds dharma and the reputation that comes with it.

### Śloka 13 · Aspected by Śukra

> भूषणयानगृहाणां शयनासनगन्धवस्त्रमाल्यानाम् । भागिनमुपभोक्तारं सितेक्षितो गवि शशी कुरुते ॥१३॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.13)*
>
> *bhūṣaṇayānagṛhāṇāṃ śayanāsanagandhavastramālyānām | bhāginamupabhoktāraṃ sitekṣito gavi śaśī kurute || 13 ||*

**Key words:** *bhūṣaṇa* ornaments · *yāna* vehicles · *gṛha* houses · *śayana* beds · *āsana* seats · *gandha* perfumes · *vastra* clothes · *mālya* garlands · *bhāgin* owner · *upabhoktā* one who enjoys · *sita* the white one (Śukra)

**Meaning:** He owns and enjoys ornaments, vehicles, houses, beds, seats, perfumes, clothes and garlands.

**Explanation:** Śukra aspects the Moon in its own sign, so the result is a catalogue of Venusian comforts. Notice the two words: *bhāgin* (he owns them) and *upabhoktā* (he actually enjoys them), which a lesser placement may separate.

### Śloka 14 · Aspected by Śani

> धनहीनमनिष्टकरं वृषभे मातुस्तथा च युवतीनाम् । सुतमित्रबन्धुसहितं रविसुतदृष्टः शशी कुरुते ॥१४॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.14)*
>
> *dhanahīnamaniṣṭakaraṃ vṛṣabhe mātustathā ca yuvatīnām | sutamitrabandhusahitaṃ ravisutadṛṣṭaḥ śaśī kurute || 14 ||*

**Key words:** *dhanahīna* without wealth · *aniṣṭakara* causing harm · *mātuḥ* to the mother · *yuvatīnām* to women (his wife) · *suta-mitra-bandhu* sons, friends, relatives · *ravisuta* son of the Sun (Śani)

**Meaning:** He is short of money and brings harm to his mother and to the women of his life, yet he has sons, friends and relatives around him.

**Explanation:** Śani is a friend of Śukra, so the aspect is mixed rather than ruinous. It dries up the Moon's wealth and its feminine significations (mother, wife), but the fixed sign keeps the wider circle of family and friends intact.

### Śloka 15 · Special rule: which half of Vṛṣabha

This verse is not about an aspect, but it closes the Vṛṣabha group, so it is included here.

> पूर्वार्धे सम्भूतो जननीमृत्युं करोति न चिरेण । पश्चादर्धे वृषभे पितुर्वियोगं शशी कुरुते ॥१५॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.15)*
>
> *pūrvārdhe sambhūto jananīmṛtyuṃ karoti na cireṇa | paścādardhe vṛṣabhe piturviyogaṃ śaśī kurute || 15 ||*

**Key words:** *pūrvārdha* first half (0°–15°) · *jananīmṛtyu* death of the mother · *na cireṇa* before long · *paścādardha* second half (15°–30°) · *viyoga* separation

**Meaning:** If the Moon is in the first half of Vṛṣabha, it soon brings the mother's death. In the second half, it brings separation from the father.

**Explanation:** Read this as a rule for afflicted cases, not a blanket verdict. The first half of the sign is tied to the mother (the Moon's own side) and the second half to the father. It should be weighed only when the Moon is otherwise weak or afflicted.

## 3. Moon in Mithuna (ślokas 17–22)

In Mithuna, Budha's airy sign, the aspects mostly turn into intellect and authority. Mars here is unusually good, giving courage with comfort. The Sun gives wisdom without money, and Saturn gives poverty and isolation.

### Śloka 17 · Aspected by Sūrya

> प्रज्ञाधनं प्रकाशं मिथुने रूपान्वितं सुधर्मिष्ठम् । अतिदुःखितमल्पार्थं करोति सूर्येक्षितश्चन्द्रः ॥१७॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.17)*
>
> *prajñādhanaṃ prakāśaṃ mithune rūpānvitaṃ sudharmiṣṭham | atiduḥkhitamalpārthaṃ karoti sūryekṣitaścandraḥ || 17 ||*

**Key words:** *prajñādhana* whose wealth is wisdom · *prakāśa* well known · *rūpānvita* handsome · *sudharmiṣṭha* deeply righteous · *atiduḥkhita* very unhappy · *alpārtha* of little means

**Meaning:** He is rich in wisdom, well known, handsome and deeply righteous, yet very unhappy and of small means.

**Explanation:** The Sun lights up the Moon in a sign of the intellect, so learning, visibility and principle are strong. But the Sun aspecting the Moon is also the lunar month at its darkest point (new Moon), which drains material comfort: the rich mind sits beside an empty purse.

### Śloka 18 · Aspected by Maṅgala

> अतिशूरमतिप्राज्ञं सुखवाहनविभवरूपसम्पन्नम् । कुरुते मिथुने चन्द्रो वक्रेण निरीक्षितोऽवश्यम् ॥१८॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.18)*
>
> *atiśūramatiprājñaṃ sukhavāhanavibhavarūpasampannam | kurute mithune candro vakreṇa nirīkṣito'vaśyam || 18 ||*

**Key words:** *atiśūra* very brave · *atiprājña* very wise · *sukha* happiness · *vāhana* vehicles · *vibhava* prosperity · *rūpa* good looks · *vakra* the crooked one (Maṅgala) · *avaśyam* certainly

**Meaning:** He is certainly very brave and very wise, and has happiness, vehicles, wealth and good looks.

**Explanation:** Mars gives the courage that an airy, thinking sign lacks, and Mithuna gives Mars a mind to direct it. The word *avaśyam* ("certainly") is the author's emphasis that this combination does not fail.

### Śloka 19 · Aspected by Budha

> अर्थोत्पादनकुशलं कुरुते ह्यपराजितं सुधीरं च । पार्थिवमखण्डिताज्ञं मिथुने बुधवीक्षितश्चन्द्रः ॥१९॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.19)*
>
> *arthotpādanakuśalaṃ kurute hyaparājitaṃ sudhīraṃ ca | pārthivamakhaṇḍitājñaṃ mithune budhavīkṣitaścandraḥ || 19 ||*

**Key words:** *arthotpādana* producing wealth · *kuśala* skilled · *aparājita* unconquered · *sudhīra* very steady · *pārthiva* ruler · *akhaṇḍitājña* whose orders are never broken

**Meaning:** He is skilled at making money, undefeated, very steady, and a ruler whose commands are never disobeyed.

**Explanation:** Budha aspects the Moon in its own sign, so Mercury's commercial cleverness becomes the native's main strength. Wealth comes through trade and planning, and the authority that follows rests on competence.

### Śloka 20 · Aspected by Guru

> विद्याशास्त्राचार्यं विख्यातं सत्यवाचमतिरूपम् । मान्यं वाग्मिनमिन्दुः करोति गुरुवीक्षितो मिथुने ॥२०॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.20)*
>
> *vidyāśāstrācāryaṃ vikhyātaṃ satyavācamatirūpam | mānyaṃ vāgminaminduḥ karoti guruvīkṣito mithune || 20 ||*

**Key words:** *vidyā-śāstra-ācārya* teacher of the sciences and śāstras · *vikhyāta* famous · *satyavāc* truthful · *atirūpa* very handsome · *mānya* respected · *vāgmin* eloquent

**Meaning:** He becomes a famous teacher of learning and the śāstras, truthful, very handsome, respected and eloquent.

**Explanation:** Guru aspects the Moon from Dhanu, the sign opposite Mithuna, so knowledge (Mithuna) is joined to wisdom and tradition (Dhanu). That axis produces the ācārya rather than the mere scholar.

### Śloka 21 · Aspected by Śukra

> वरयुवतिमाल्यवस्त्रैर्वरवाहनयानभूषणैर्मणिभिः । क्रीडां कुरुते पुरुषो भृगुदृष्टे शशिनि मिथुनस्थे ॥२१॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.21)*
>
> *varayuvatimālyavastrairvaravāhanayānabhūṣaṇairmaṇibhiḥ | krīḍāṃ kurute puruṣo bhṛgudṛṣṭe śaśini mithunasthe || 21 ||*

**Key words:** *varayuvati* fine young women · *mālya* garlands · *vastra* clothes · *vāhana, yāna* mounts and carriages · *bhūṣaṇa* ornaments · *maṇi* gems · *krīḍā* play, enjoyment

**Meaning:** He takes his pleasure among fine women, garlands, clothes, excellent vehicles and carriages, ornaments and gems.

**Explanation:** Mithuna is a playful sign, and Śukra's aspect makes life itself a kind of play (*krīḍā*). Compare Vṛṣabha (śloka 13), where the same comforts are owned and settled; here they are enjoyed and moved through.

### Śloka 22 · Aspected by Śani

> कुरुते बान्धवरहितं युवतिसुखविभूतिवर्जितं चापि । अधनं लोकद्वेष्यं जितुमे शनिनेक्षितश्चन्द्रः ॥२२॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.22)*
>
> *kurute bāndhavarahitaṃ yuvatisukhavibhūtivarjitaṃ cāpi | adhanaṃ lokadveṣyaṃ jitume śaninekṣitaścandraḥ || 22 ||*

**Key words:** *bāndhavarahita* without relatives · *yuvatisukha* happiness from women · *vibhūti* prosperity · *varjita* deprived of · *adhana* poor · *lokadveṣya* disliked by people · *jituma* Gemini (a Greek-derived name)

**Meaning:** He is without relatives, deprived of joy from women and of prosperity, poor and disliked by people.

**Explanation:** Saturn separates. On the Moon in a sign about connection and exchange, it cuts the native off from family, partners and society. Note *jituma*, the Greek loan-word for Gemini that Sārāvalī sometimes uses instead of Mithuna.

## 4. Moon in Karka (ślokas 24–29)

Karka is the Moon's own sign, so the Moon is strong enough to carry whatever the aspect brings. Mercury and Jupiter give office and kingship. Mars and Saturn hurt the body and the mother, and the Sun gives service rather than rule.

### Śloka 24 · Aspected by Sūrya

> नरपतिपुरुषमधन्यं धनरहितं लेखहारकं वाऽपि । कुरुते स्वगृहे चन्द्रो रविदृष्टो दुर्गपालं च ॥२४॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.24)*
>
> *narapatipuruṣamadhanyaṃ dhanarahitaṃ lekhahārakaṃ vā'pi | kurute svagṛhe candro ravidṛṣṭo durgapālaṃ ca || 24 ||*

**Key words:** *narapatipuruṣa* a king's servant · *adhanya* unlucky · *dhanarahita* without wealth · *lekhahāraka* letter-carrier, messenger · *durgapāla* keeper of a fort

**Meaning:** He becomes a king's servant, unlucky and without wealth, perhaps a messenger, or the keeper of a fort.

**Explanation:** The Sun is the king, and the Moon in its own home is the subject. The aspect places the native close to power but in its service: carrying its letters, guarding its forts. Proximity to authority does not bring wealth.

### Śloka 25 · Aspected by Maṅgala

> शूरं विकलशरीरं मातुरनर्थावहं क्रियादक्षम् । क्षितितनयवीक्षिततनुर्जनयति चन्द्रो नरं स्वगृहे ॥२५॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.25)*
>
> *śūraṃ vikalaśarīraṃ māturanarthāvahaṃ kriyādakṣam | kṣititanayavīkṣitatanurjanayati candro naraṃ svagṛhe || 25 ||*

**Key words:** *śūra* brave · *vikalaśarīra* with an impaired body · *anarthāvaha* bringing misfortune · *kriyādakṣa* skilled in action · *kṣititanaya* son of the Earth (Maṅgala)

**Meaning:** He is brave and capable in action, but has some bodily defect and brings trouble to his mother.

**Explanation:** Mars is debilitated in Karka, so its aspect on the Moon there carries a wound: injury or impairment of the body and strain on the mother. Mars still gives courage and practical skill.

### Śloka 26 · Aspected by Budha

> अविकलमतिं नयज्ञं जनयति बुधवीक्षितः शशी स्वगृहे । धनदारपुत्रवन्तं नृपसचिवं सौख्यवन्तं च ॥२६॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.26)*
>
> *avikalamatiṃ nayajñaṃ janayati budhavīkṣitaḥ śaśī svagṛhe | dhanadāraputravantaṃ nṛpasacivaṃ saukhyavantaṃ ca || 26 ||*

**Key words:** *avikalamati* of sound mind · *nayajña* knowing statecraft · *dhana-dāra-putra* wealth, wife, sons · *nṛpasaciva* king's minister · *saukhyavant* happy

**Meaning:** He has a sound mind and knows policy. He has wealth, wife and sons, serves as a king's minister, and is happy.

**Explanation:** Budha's aspect clarifies the emotional Karka Moon into judgement (*naya*, policy). The strong Moon supplies family and comfort; Mercury supplies the counsellor's role.

### Śloka 27 · Aspected by Guru

> नृपतिं नृपगुणयुक्तं जनयति चन्द्रः सुरेज्यसंदृष्टः । स्वगृहे सुखितसुभार्यं नयविनयपराक्रमाक्रान्तम् ॥२७॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.27)*
>
> *nṛpatiṃ nṛpaguṇayuktaṃ janayati candraḥ surejyasaṃdṛṣṭaḥ | svagṛhe sukhitasubhāryaṃ nayavinayaparākramākrāntam || 27 ||*

**Key words:** *nṛpati* king · *nṛpaguṇa* royal qualities · *surejya* the gods' priest (Guru) · *subhārya* with a good wife · *naya-vinaya-parākrama* policy, modesty and valour

**Meaning:** He becomes a king with royal qualities, happy, with a good wife, and full of good policy, humility and courage.

**Explanation:** Guru is exalted in Karka, and its aspect on the Moon in its own sign is the strongest combination in this group: the full *gaja-kesarī* feeling of Moon and Jupiter, which here reaches kingship.

### Śloka 28 · Aspected by Śukra

> धनकनकवस्त्रयोषिद्रत्नानां भाजनं शशी कुरुते । कर्कटके सितदृष्टो वेश्याजननायकं कान्तम् ॥२८॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.28)*
>
> *dhanakanakavastrayoṣidratnānāṃ bhājanaṃ śaśī kurute | karkaṭake sitadṛṣṭo veśyājananāyakaṃ kāntam || 28 ||*

**Key words:** *dhana* wealth · *kanaka* gold · *vastra* clothes · *yoṣit* women · *ratna* gems · *bhājana* vessel, receptacle · *veśyājana-nāyaka* leader among courtesans · *kānta* handsome

**Meaning:** He becomes a vessel for wealth, gold, clothes, women and gems. He is handsome, and a favourite among courtesans.

**Explanation:** Venus aspecting a strong, watery Moon gives abundance and charm. The *veśyā* phrase points to pleasures outside marriage: Venus and Moon together in this way tilt toward indulgence.

### Śloka 29 · Aspected by Śani

> अटनमसुखं दरिद्रं मातुरनिष्टं प्रियानृतं पापम् । शनिना दृष्टः स्वगृहे करोति चन्द्रो नरं नीचम् ॥२९॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.29)*
>
> *aṭanamasukhaṃ daridraṃ māturaniṣṭaṃ priyānṛtaṃ pāpam | śaninā dṛṣṭaḥ svagṛhe karoti candro naraṃ nīcam || 29 ||*

**Key words:** *aṭana* a wanderer · *asukha* unhappy · *daridra* poor · *mātuḥ aniṣṭa* harmful to the mother · *priyānṛta* fond of lies · *pāpa* sinful · *nīca* low

**Meaning:** He wanders, is unhappy and poor, harmful to his mother, fond of lies, sinful and low.

**Explanation:** Saturn aspects from Makara, the sign opposite, so the axis of home (Karka) and duty (Makara) is set against the native. The Moon cannot stay home (*aṭana*, wandering), and the mother suffers.

## 5. Moon in Siṃha (ślokas 31–36)

Siṃha is the Sun's royal sign, so most aspects here produce rank: king-like (Sun, Jupiter) or a commander (Mars). Mercury and Venus bend the native toward women. Saturn gives the same lowly fort-keeper as in Karka.

### Śloka 31 · Aspected by Sūrya

> नृपतिसमत्वं कुरुते प्रकृष्टगुणं महास्वनं वीरम् । रविणा दृष्टः सिंहे पानरतं विश्रुतं चन्द्रः ॥३१॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.31)*
>
> *nṛpatisamatvaṃ kurute prakṛṣṭaguṇaṃ mahāsvanaṃ vīram | raviṇā dṛṣṭaḥ siṃhe pānarataṃ viśrutaṃ candraḥ || 31 ||*

**Key words:** *nṛpatisamatva* equality with a king · *prakṛṣṭaguṇa* of excellent qualities · *mahāsvana* loud-voiced · *vīra* hero · *pānarata* fond of drink · *viśruta* famous

**Meaning:** He becomes the equal of a king, with excellent qualities, a commanding voice and heroism. He is famous, and fond of drinking.

**Explanation:** The Sun aspects the Moon in its own sign, so the native's mind takes on solar stature: presence, voice, reputation. Leo's appetite for celebration shows up as *pānarata*.

### Śloka 32 · Aspected by Maṅgala

> सेनापतिं प्रचण्डं वरयुवतिसुतार्थवाहनोपेतम् । जनयत्युत्तमपुरुषं कुजेक्षितश्चन्द्रमाः सिंहे ॥३२॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.32)*
>
> *senāpatiṃ pracaṇḍaṃ varayuvatisutārthavāhanopetam | janayatyuttamapuruṣaṃ kujekṣitaścandramāḥ siṃhe || 32 ||*

**Key words:** *senāpati* commander of an army · *pracaṇḍa* fierce · *varayuvati* a fine wife · *suta* sons · *artha* wealth · *vāhana* vehicles · *uttamapuruṣa* an excellent man

**Meaning:** He becomes a fierce army commander, an excellent man with a fine wife, sons, wealth and vehicles.

**Explanation:** Mars is a friend of the Sun and is at home in a fiery royal sign. Its aspect turns the Moon's authority into command in the field, and, unlike in Karka, it does not harm the body.

### Śloka 33 · Aspected by Budha

> स्त्रीसत्त्वं स्त्रीललितं स्त्रीवश्यं युवतिसेवकं सिंहे । कुरुते बुधेन दृष्टो धनसुखभोगान्वितं चन्द्रः ॥३३॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.33)*
>
> *strīsattvaṃ strīlalitaṃ strīvaśyaṃ yuvatisevakaṃ siṃhe | kurute budhena dṛṣṭo dhanasukhabhogānvitaṃ candraḥ || 33 ||*

**Key words:** *strīsattva* of feminine temperament · *strīlalita* graceful like a woman · *strīvaśya* controlled by women · *yuvatisevaka* attending on women · *dhana-sukha-bhoga* wealth, happiness, pleasures

**Meaning:** He has a gentle, womanly temperament and grace, is ruled by women and devoted to serving them, and enjoys wealth, happiness and pleasures.

**Explanation:** Budha is neuter and adaptable. Its aspect softens the lion's sign into charm and accommodation, so the native gains through gentleness and through women rather than through dominance.

### Śloka 34 · Aspected by Guru

> अभिजातं कुलमुख्यं बहुश्रुतं गुणसमृद्धं च । कुरुते नरेन्द्रतुल्यं गुरुदृष्टश्चन्द्रमाः सिंहे ॥३४॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.34)*
>
> *abhijātaṃ kulamukhyaṃ bahuśrutaṃ guṇasamṛddhaṃ ca | kurute narendratulyaṃ gurudṛṣṭaścandramāḥ siṃhe || 34 ||*

**Key words:** *abhijāta* well-born · *kulamukhya* head of the family · *bahuśruta* widely learned · *guṇasamṛddha* rich in virtues · *narendratulya* like a king

**Meaning:** He is well-born, the head of his family, widely learned, rich in virtues, and like a king.

**Explanation:** Guru, the Sun's friend, aspects from a trine (Dhanu or Meṣa) or from Kumbha. It turns royal status into dignity earned by learning and lineage: the respected patriarch rather than the warlord.

### Śloka 35 · Aspected by Śukra

> प्रमदाविभवैर्युक्तं रोगिणमपि युवतिसेवकं कुरुते । सुरतविधिज्ञं प्राज्ञं शशी हरौ शुक्रसन्दृष्टः ॥३५॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.35)*
>
> *pramadāvibhavairyuktaṃ rogiṇamapi yuvatisevakaṃ kurute | suratavidhijñaṃ prājñaṃ śaśī harau śukrasandṛṣṭaḥ || 35 ||*

**Key words:** *pramadā* women · *vibhava* wealth · *rogin* sickly · *yuvatisevaka* devoted to women · *suratavidhijña* versed in the art of love · *prājña* wise · *hari* the lion (Siṃha)

**Meaning:** He has women and wealth, is devoted to women and versed in the art of love, and is wise, but also sickly.

**Explanation:** Śukra is an enemy of the Sun. Its aspect brings pleasure into Leo's house, but the clash shows as illness. Note *hari*, a poetic name for Siṃha.

### Śloka 36 · Aspected by Śani

> कर्षकमधनं कुरुतेऽनृतवाचं दुर्गपालकं सिंहे । रविजेन तथा दृष्टो युवतिसुखैर्हीनमल्पकं च शशी ॥३६॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.36)*
>
> *karṣakamadhanaṃ kurute'nṛtavācaṃ durgapālakaṃ siṃhe | ravijena tathā dṛṣṭo yuvatisukhairhīnamalpakaṃ ca śaśī || 36 ||*

**Key words:** *karṣaka* cultivator · *adhana* poor · *anṛtavāc* untruthful · *durgapālaka* keeper of a fort · *ravija* son of the Sun (Śani) · *yuvatisukha* joy from women · *alpaka* insignificant

**Meaning:** He works the land, is poor and untruthful, keeps a fort, lacks the joys of women, and counts for little.

**Explanation:** Saturn, the Sun's enemy, aspecting the Moon in the Sun's sign undoes Leo's dignity: the native serves (fort-keeper, farmer) where others rule. The same *durgapāla* appeared in śloka 24 with the Sun on the Moon in Karka.

## 6. Moon in Kanyā (ślokas 38–43)

Kanyā is Budha's earthy sign of skill and service, so the aspects here give specialised competence: treasury (Sun), crafts (Mars), astrology and debate (Mercury). Several aspects cost the native a wife or the mother. Saturn leaves him dependent on a woman's money.

### Śloka 38 · Aspected by Sūrya

> नृपकोशकरं ख्यातं गृहीतवाक्यं विशिष्टकर्माणम् । कन्यायां रविदृष्टो भार्याहीनं शशी कुरुते ॥३८॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.38)*
>
> *nṛpakośakaraṃ khyātaṃ gṛhītavākyaṃ viśiṣṭakarmāṇam | kanyāyāṃ ravidṛṣṭo bhāryāhīnaṃ śaśī kurute || 38 ||*

**Key words:** *nṛpakośakara* keeper of the king's treasury · *khyāta* well known · *gṛhītavākya* whose word is accepted · *viśiṣṭakarman* of distinguished work · *bhāryāhīna* without a wife

**Meaning:** He becomes the keeper of the king's treasury, well known, a man whose word is accepted and whose work stands out, but he is without a wife.

**Explanation:** The Sun brings royal service, and Kanyā brings accounts and precision, so the result is the treasurer. The Sun on the Moon in this "virgin" sign also denies or delays a wife.

### Śloka 39 · Aspected by Maṅgala

> शिल्पाचार्यं ख्यातं धनवन्तं शिक्षितं सुधीरं च । कन्यायां कुजदृष्टो मातुरनिष्टं शशी कुरुते ॥३९॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.39)*
>
> *śilpācāryaṃ khyātaṃ dhanavantaṃ śikṣitaṃ sudhīraṃ ca | kanyāyāṃ kujadṛṣṭo māturaniṣṭaṃ śaśī kurute || 39 ||*

**Key words:** *śilpācārya* master of the crafts · *dhanavant* wealthy · *śikṣita* trained, educated · *sudhīra* very steady · *mātuḥ aniṣṭa* harmful to the mother

**Meaning:** He becomes a famous master craftsman, wealthy, well trained and steady, but harmful to his mother.

**Explanation:** Mars gives the hands and Kanyā gives the method: together they make the engineer or craftsman (*śilpa*). As in Karka, Mars on the Moon strains the mother.

### Śloka 40 · Aspected by Budha

> ज्योतिषकाव्यविधिज्ञं विवादकलहेषु विजयिनं सुतराम् । सातिशयं कन्यायां जनयति निपुणं बुधेक्षितश्चन्द्रः ॥४०॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.40)*
>
> *jyotiṣakāvyavidhijñaṃ vivādakalaheṣu vijayinaṃ sutarām | sātiśayaṃ kanyāyāṃ janayati nipuṇaṃ budhekṣitaścandraḥ || 40 ||*

**Key words:** *jyotiṣa* astrology · *kāvya* poetry · *vidhijña* knowing the rules of · *vivāda-kalaha* debates and quarrels · *vijayin* victorious · *sātiśaya* exceptional · *nipuṇa* expert

**Meaning:** He knows the rules of astrology and poetry, wins every debate and dispute, and is exceptionally skilled.

**Explanation:** Budha is exalted in Kanyā, so its aspect on the Moon there is Mercury at full power: calculation (Jyotiṣa), language (kāvya) and argument. This is one of the classical signatures for an astrologer.

### Śloka 41 · Aspected by Guru

> बन्धुजनाढ्यं सुखिनं नृपकृत्यकरं गृहीतवाक्यं च । कन्यायां गुरुदृष्टो जनयति विभवान्वितं चन्द्रः ॥४१॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.41)*
>
> *bandhujanāḍhyaṃ sukhinaṃ nṛpakṛtyakaraṃ gṛhītavākyaṃ ca | kanyāyāṃ gurudṛṣṭo janayati vibhavānvitaṃ candraḥ || 41 ||*

**Key words:** *bandhujanāḍhya* rich in relatives · *sukhin* happy · *nṛpakṛtyakara* doing the king's work · *gṛhītavākya* whose word is heeded · *vibhavānvita* prosperous

**Meaning:** He has many relatives, is happy, carries out the king's business, is listened to, and is prosperous.

**Explanation:** Guru aspects from the opposite sign Mīna, its own, or from its trines. It adds family and contentment to Kanyā's competence, and keeps the native in trusted government work.

### Śloka 42 · Aspected by Śukra

> कन्यायां बहुदारं विविधालङ्कारभोगिनमथाढ्यम् । सततमिहोर्जितमुदितं कुरुते भृगुणा निरीक्षितश्चन्द्रः ॥४२॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.42)*
>
> *kanyāyāṃ bahudāraṃ vividhālaṅkārabhoginamathāḍhyam | satatamihorjitamuditaṃ kurute bhṛguṇā nirīkṣitaścandraḥ || 42 ||*

**Key words:** *bahudāra* with many wives · *vividhālaṅkāra* many kinds of ornaments · *bhogin* enjoying · *āḍhya* rich · *ūrjita* vigorous, flourishing · *mudita* joyful

**Meaning:** He has many wives, enjoys all kinds of ornaments, is rich, and is always flourishing and cheerful.

**Explanation:** Śukra is debilitated in Kanyā, yet its aspect on the Moon there is pleasant: the debilitation shows not as poverty but as excess (*bahudāra*, many partners). Compare śloka 38, where the Sun gave no wife at all.

### Śloka 43 · Aspected by Śani

> अदृढस्मृतिं दरिद्रं सुखरहितममातृकं युवतिवश्यम् । कन्यायां यमदृष्टः स्त्रीभागधनं शशी कुरुते ॥४३॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.43)*
>
> *adṛḍhasmṛtiṃ daridraṃ sukharahitamāmātṛkaṃ yuvativaśyam | kanyāyāṃ yamadṛṣṭaḥ strībhāgadhanaṃ śaśī kurute || 43 ||*

**Key words:** *adṛḍhasmṛti* of weak memory · *daridra* poor · *sukharahita* unhappy · *amātṛka* motherless · *yuvativaśya* controlled by women · *yama* Saturn · *strībhāgadhana* living on a woman's share of wealth

**Meaning:** He has a poor memory, is poor and unhappy, loses his mother, is ruled by women, and lives on a woman's money.

**Explanation:** Saturn on the Mercury-ruled Moon weakens memory and learning (the opposite of śloka 40). The loss of the mother and dependence on a wife's wealth show Saturn cutting the Moon's support. Note *yama*, a name for Saturn as the son of the Sun.

## 7. Moon in Tulā (ślokas 45–50)

Tulā is Śukra's sign of trade and balance, and the dignity of each aspecting graha decides the result. The Sun (debilitated here) is the worst aspect, and Saturn (exalted here) is surprisingly good. Mercury and Jupiter give commerce and honour.

### Śloka 45 · Aspected by Sūrya

> अधनं व्याधितमटनं परिभूतं भोगविप्रयुक्तं च । असुतमसारं जूके जनयति रविवीक्षितश्चन्द्रः ॥४५॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.45)*
>
> *adhanaṃ vyādhitamaṭanaṃ paribhūtaṃ bhogaviprayuktaṃ ca | asutamasāraṃ jūke janayati ravivīkṣitaścandraḥ || 45 ||*

**Key words:** *adhana* poor · *vyādhita* sick · *aṭana* wandering · *paribhūta* humiliated · *bhogaviprayukta* cut off from enjoyment · *asuta* childless · *asāra* without substance · *jūka* Libra (a Greek-derived name)

**Meaning:** He is poor, sick, a wanderer, humiliated, cut off from enjoyment, childless and of no substance.

**Explanation:** The Sun is debilitated in Tulā, so its aspect carries the Sun's weakness into the mind: loss of standing (*paribhūta*), of health and of progeny, which are all solar significations. *Jūka* is Sārāvalī's Greek loan-word for Libra.

### Śloka 46 · Aspected by Maṅgala

> तीक्ष्णं चोरं क्षुद्रं परयोषिद्गन्धमाल्यसंयुक्तम् । मतिमन्नयनातुरगं [?] जनयति वक्रेक्षितश्चन्द्रः ॥४६॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.46)*
>
> *tīkṣṇaṃ coraṃ kṣudraṃ parayoṣidgandhamālyasaṃyuktam | matimannayanāturagaṃ [?] janayati vakrekṣitaścandraḥ || 46 ||*

**Key words:** *tīkṣṇa* sharp, harsh · *cora* thief · *kṣudra* petty · *parayoṣit* another man's wife · *gandha-mālya* perfumes and garlands · *matimat* clever · *nayanātura* suffering in the eyes · *vakra* Maṅgala

**Meaning:** He is harsh, a thief and petty, involved with other men's wives, fond of perfumes and garlands. He is clever, but suffers from eye trouble.

**Explanation:** Mars in Venus's sign of partnership turns desire toward what belongs to others: property (*cora*) and spouses (*parayoṣit*). The print of the second line is unclear; I read it as *matimat* + *nayanātura* (clever, and troubled in the eyes).

### Śloka 47 · Aspected by Budha

> दृष्टो बुधेन चन्द्रः कलाविदग्धं प्रभूतधनधान्यम् । शुभवाक्यं विद्वांसं देशख्यातं तुलाधरे कुरुते ॥४७॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.47)*
>
> *dṛṣṭo budhena candraḥ kalāvidagdhaṃ prabhūtadhanadhānyam | śubhavākyaṃ vidvāṃsaṃ deśakhyātaṃ tulādhare kurute || 47 ||*

**Key words:** *kalāvidagdha* accomplished in the arts · *prabhūta* abundant · *dhana-dhānya* money and grain · *śubhavākya* of pleasant speech · *vidvān* learned · *deśakhyāta* famous in his country · *tulādhara* the Balance

**Meaning:** He is accomplished in the arts, has abundant money and grain, speaks pleasantly, is learned and famous throughout his country.

**Explanation:** Budha is a friend of Śukra and aspects from a trine (Kumbha or Mithuna) or from Meṣa. It refines Libra's sense of beauty into skill in the arts, and its trade sense into stores of wealth.

### Śloka 48 · Aspected by Guru

> जीवेक्षितस्तुलायां जनयति सर्वत्र पूजितं हिमगुः । क्रयविक्रयेषु कुशलं रत्नादिषु भाण्डजातेषु ॥४८॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.48)*
>
> *jīvekṣitastulāyāṃ janayati sarvatra pūjitaṃ himaguḥ | krayavikrayeṣu kuśalaṃ ratnādiṣu bhāṇḍajāteṣu || 48 ||*

**Key words:** *jīva* Guru · *sarvatra pūjita* honoured everywhere · *himagu* cool-rayed (Moon) · *krayavikraya* buying and selling · *ratna* gems · *bhāṇḍajāta* all kinds of goods

**Meaning:** He is honoured everywhere and skilled in buying and selling gems and goods of every kind.

**Explanation:** Libra is the scale of the market. Guru's aspect makes the native's trade honest and respected, so commerce brings honour (*pūjita*) as well as profit. Gems are a typical Venus-Jupiter trade.

### Śloka 49 · Aspected by Śukra

> ललितमरोगं सुभगं समुपचिताङ्गं धनान्वितं प्राज्ञम् । विविधोपायविधिज्ञं कुरुते भृगुवीक्षितः शशी तुलके ॥४९॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.49)*
>
> *lalitamarogaṃ subhagaṃ samupacitāṅgaṃ dhanānvitaṃ prājñam | vividhopāyavidhijñaṃ kurute bhṛguvīkṣitaḥ śaśī tulake || 49 ||*

**Key words:** *lalita* graceful · *aroga* healthy · *subhaga* fortunate, charming · *samupacitāṅga* well-built · *dhanānvita* wealthy · *vividhopāyavidhijña* knowing many ways and means

**Meaning:** He is graceful, healthy, charming, well-built, wealthy and wise, and knows many ways of getting things done.

**Explanation:** Śukra aspects the Moon in its own mūlatrikoṇa sign. Grace, health and resourcefulness (*upāya*, diplomacy) are Libra's best gifts, given here in full.

### Śloka 50 · Aspected by Śani

> कुरुते शशी धनाढ्यं प्रियवाक्यं वाहनैर्युतं जूके । विषयरतिं सुखरहितं भास्करिदृष्टो हितं मातुः ॥५०॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.50)*
>
> *kurute śaśī dhanāḍhyaṃ priyavākyaṃ vāhanairyutaṃ jūke | viṣayaratiṃ sukharahitaṃ bhāskaridṛṣṭo hitaṃ mātuḥ || 50 ||*

**Key words:** *dhanāḍhya* rich · *priyavākya* sweet-spoken · *vāhana* vehicles · *viṣayarati* attached to sense pleasures · *sukharahita* without (inner) happiness · *bhāskari* son of the Sun (Śani) · *hitaṃ mātuḥ* good for the mother

**Meaning:** He is rich, sweet-spoken and has vehicles. He is drawn to sense pleasures but lacks happiness, and he is good for his mother.

**Explanation:** Saturn is exalted in Tulā, so its aspect here is the only Saturn aspect in the chapter that brings wealth and helps the mother. Saturn still withholds contentment: comfort without joy.

## 8. Moon in Vṛścika (ślokas 52–57)

Vṛścika is the Moon's debilitation sign and Maṅgala's own, so the aspects here are sharper and more mixed than elsewhere. Mars, at home, gives the best result: kingly courage. Even Jupiter and the Sun leave the native disliked by people, and Saturn is harsh.

### Śloka 52 · Aspected by Sūrya

> कुरुते लोकद्वेष्यं बुधमटनं चैव वित्तवन्तं च । दिनकरदृष्टोऽलिगतश्चन्द्रः सुखवर्जितं पुरुषम् ॥५२॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.52)*
>
> *kurute lokadveṣyaṃ budhamaṭanaṃ caiva vittavantaṃ ca | dinakaradṛṣṭo'ligataścandraḥ sukhavarjitaṃ puruṣam || 52 ||*

**Key words:** *lokadveṣya* at odds with people · *budha* (here) learned · *aṭana* wandering · *vittavant* wealthy · *ali* the scorpion (Vṛścika) · *sukhavarjita* without happiness

**Meaning:** He is at odds with people, learned, a wanderer, and wealthy, but without happiness.

**Explanation:** The Sun is a friend of Mars and gives learning and money in a Mars sign. But on a debilitated Moon, it makes the native proud and isolated: respected for his knowledge, not liked for himself.

### Śloka 53 · Aspected by Maṅgala

> अनुपमधैर्यं कुरुते नृपतिसमं वृश्चिके विभूतियुतम् । शूरमजय्यं समरे प्रभक्षणं भूमिजेन संदृष्टः ॥५३॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.53)*
>
> *anupamadhairyaṃ kurute nṛpatisamaṃ vṛścike vibhūtiyutam | śūramajayyaṃ samare prabhakṣaṇaṃ bhūmijena saṃdṛṣṭaḥ || 53 ||*

**Key words:** *anupamadhairya* of matchless courage · *nṛpatisama* equal to a king · *vibhūtiyuta* prosperous · *ajayya samare* unbeatable in battle · *prabhakṣaṇa* a big eater · *bhūmija* born of the Earth (Maṅgala)

**Meaning:** He has matchless courage, is equal to a king and prosperous, brave and unbeatable in battle, and eats heartily.

**Explanation:** Mars aspects the Moon in its own sign, and this protects the debilitated Moon: the dispositor's strength lifts the Moon. The rule in śloka 86 (a strong sign lord makes the results fully good) is visible here.

### Śloka 54 · Aspected by Budha

> अचतुरममृष्टवाक्यं यमलापत्यं च युक्तिमन्तं च । जनयति बुधेन दृष्टः कूटकरं वृश्चिके च गीतज्ञम् ॥५४॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.54)*
>
> *acaturamamṛṣṭavākyaṃ yamalāpatyaṃ ca yuktimantaṃ ca | janayati budhena dṛṣṭaḥ kūṭakaraṃ vṛścike ca gītajñam || 54 ||*

**Key words:** *acatura* unskilled · *amṛṣṭavākya* rough-spoken · *yamalāpatya* having twins · *yuktimant* resourceful · *kūṭakara* forger, fabricator · *gītajña* knowing music

**Meaning:** He is unpolished and rough in speech, may have twins, is resourceful, makes counterfeit things, and knows music.

**Explanation:** Budha is an enemy of the Moon. In a secretive water sign, its cleverness turns into cunning (*kūṭa*, the false or forged), while its artistic side survives as music. Twins (*yamala*) are a Mercury signature.

### Śloka 55 · Aspected by Guru

> कर्मासक्तं कुरुते लोकद्वेष्यं च वित्तवन्तं च । गुरुणा दृष्टोऽलिगतो निशाकरो रूपवन्तं च ॥५५॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.55)*
>
> *karmāsaktaṃ kurute lokadveṣyaṃ ca vittavantaṃ ca | guruṇā dṛṣṭo'ligato niśākaro rūpavantaṃ ca || 55 ||*

**Key words:** *karmāsakta* devoted to his work · *lokadveṣya* at odds with people · *vittavant* wealthy · *rūpavant* handsome · *niśākara* maker of the night (Moon)

**Meaning:** He is devoted to his work, wealthy and handsome, but at odds with people.

**Explanation:** Guru is a friend of Mars and brings duty and money. That even Jupiter leaves *lokadveṣya* here shows the debilitated Moon's own nature: guarded, suspicious, hard for others to warm to.

### Śloka 56 · Aspected by Śukra

> अतिमतिमतीव सुभगं धनवाहनभोगललितमिह कीटे । युवतिविनाशितसारं जनयति भृगुवीक्षितश्चन्द्रः ॥५६॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.56)*
>
> *atimatimatīva subhagaṃ dhanavāhanabhogalalitamiha kīṭe | yuvativināśitasāraṃ janayati bhṛguvīkṣitaścandraḥ || 56 ||*

**Key words:** *atimati* very intelligent · *atīva subhaga* very charming · *dhana-vāhana-bhoga* wealth, vehicles, pleasures · *lalita* graceful · *kīṭa* the insect (Vṛścika) · *yuvativināśitasāra* whose vigour is wasted on women

**Meaning:** He is very intelligent and very charming, graceful, with wealth, vehicles and pleasures, but his strength is wasted on women.

**Explanation:** Venus aspects from its own sign Vṛṣabha, opposite. The 1–7 axis of desire (Scorpio–Taurus) gives charm and luxury, and also its cost: vitality spent on sensual life.

### Śloka 57 · Aspected by Śani

> नीचापत्यं कृपणं व्याधितमधनं च सत्यहीनं च । जनयत्यन्तकदृष्टो नरमधनं चन्द्रमाः कीटे ॥५७॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.57)*
>
> *nīcāpatyaṃ kṛpaṇaṃ vyādhitamadhanaṃ ca satyahīnaṃ ca | janayatyantakadṛṣṭo naramadhanaṃ candramāḥ kīṭe || 57 ||*

**Key words:** *nīcāpatya* with low or unworthy children · *kṛpaṇa* miserly · *vyādhita* sick · *adhana* poor · *satyahīna* untruthful · *antaka* the ender (Śani)

**Meaning:** His children are unworthy; he is miserly, sick, poor and untruthful.

**Explanation:** Saturn, called *antaka* ("the ender") here, aspects a debilitated Moon in a sign of hidden things: scarcity in money, health, honesty and progeny. The print repeats *adhana* in the second line (*naramadhanaṃ*); *naram adhamaṃ* ("the lowest of men") may be the intended reading [?].

## 9. Moon in Dhanu (ślokas 59–64)

Dhanu is Guru's sign of dharma, and here every aspect is favourable, Saturn's included. This is the only sign in the chapter where all six results are good. The aspects differ only in the kind of eminence: king, general, artist, minister, family man, or truthful royal officer.

### Śloka 59 · Aspected by Sūrya

> नृपतिमथाढ्यं कुरुते शूरं विख्यातपौरुषं चापे । भास्करदृष्टश्चन्द्रस्त्वनुपमसुखवाहनोपेतम् ॥५९॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.59)*
>
> *nṛpatimathāḍhyaṃ kurute śūraṃ vikhyātapauruṣaṃ cāpe | bhāskaradṛṣṭaścandrastvanupamasukhavāhanopetam || 59 ||*

**Key words:** *nṛpati* king · *āḍhya* rich · *śūra* brave · *vikhyātapauruṣa* famed for his valour · *cāpa* the bow (Dhanu) · *anupama-sukha-vāhana* matchless happiness and vehicles

**Meaning:** He becomes a king, rich, brave and famous for his valour, with matchless happiness and vehicles.

**Explanation:** The Sun is a friend of Guru and Dhanu is a fiery, royal sign. Its aspect on the Moon here gives full solar rank with none of the poverty it brings in Mithuna, the opposite sign.

### Śloka 60 · Aspected by Maṅgala

> सेनापतिं समृद्धं सुभगं प्रख्यातपौरुषं पुरुषम् । जनयत्यनुपमभृत्यं क्षितिसुतदृष्टः शशी धनुषि ॥६०॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.60)*
>
> *senāpatiṃ samṛddhaṃ subhagaṃ prakhyātapauruṣaṃ puruṣam | janayatyanupamabhṛtyaṃ kṣitisutadṛṣṭaḥ śaśī dhanuṣi || 60 ||*

**Key words:** *senāpati* army commander · *samṛddha* prosperous · *subhaga* charming, fortunate · *prakhyātapauruṣa* of renowned valour · *anupamabhṛtya* with unequalled followers

**Meaning:** He becomes a prosperous and fortunate army commander, renowned for valour, with followers no one can match.

**Explanation:** Mars, Guru's friend, aspects the Moon in the archer's sign: the warrior with a cause. As in Siṃha (śloka 32), Mars in a fire sign gives command, not injury.

### Śloka 61 · Aspected by Budha

> बहुभृत्यं त्वक्सारं ज्यौतिषशिल्पक्रियादिनिपुणं च । बुधदृष्टो हिमरश्मिर्नाट्याचार्यं हये कुरुते ॥६१॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.61)*
>
> *bahubhṛtyaṃ tvaksāraṃ jyautiṣaśilpakriyādinipuṇaṃ ca | budhadṛṣṭo himaraśmirnāṭyācāryaṃ haye kurute || 61 ||*

**Key words:** *bahubhṛtya* with many servants · *tvaksāra* whose strength lies in the skin [?] · *jyautiṣa* astrology · *śilpakriyā* crafts · *nipuṇa* expert · *nāṭyācārya* master of drama and dance · *haya* the horse (Dhanu)

**Meaning:** He has many servants, is expert in astrology, crafts and similar skills, and becomes a master of drama and dance.

**Explanation:** Budha aspects from Mithuna, the opposite sign, so learning meets performance. *Tvaksāra* is unclear in context [?]: in the classical "sāra" body-types it describes a person with fine, glowing skin, which suits a performer.

### Śloka 62 · Aspected by Guru

> अनुपमदेहं कुरुते पृथ्वीपालस्य मन्त्रिणं चापे । त्रिदशगुरुदृष्टमूर्तिर्धनधर्मसुखान्वितं चन्द्रः ॥६२॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.62)*
>
> *anupamadehaṃ kurute pṛthvīpālasya mantriṇaṃ cāpe | tridaśagurudṛṣṭamūrtirdhanadharmasukhānvitaṃ candraḥ || 62 ||*

**Key words:** *anupamadeha* of matchless physique · *pṛthvīpāla* king · *mantrin* minister · *tridaśaguru* teacher of the gods (Guru) · *dhana-dharma-sukha* wealth, righteousness, happiness

**Meaning:** He has a matchless physique and becomes a king's minister, endowed with wealth, dharma and happiness.

**Explanation:** Guru aspects the Moon in its own mūlatrikoṇa sign, the combination of mind and wisdom at its best. The result is the counsellor whose wealth and happiness rest on dharma.

### Śloka 63 · Aspected by Śukra

> सुखिनमतीव हि ललितं सुभगं पुत्रार्थकामवन्तं च । चापे सुमित्रभार्यं भार्गवदृष्टः करोतीन्दुः ॥६३॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.63)*
>
> *sukhinamatīva hi lalitaṃ subhagaṃ putrārthakāmavantaṃ ca | cāpe sumitrabhāryaṃ bhārgavadṛṣṭaḥ karotīnduḥ || 63 ||*

**Key words:** *sukhin* happy · *lalita* graceful · *subhaga* charming · *putra-artha-kāma* sons, wealth and fulfilled desires · *sumitrabhārya* with good friends and a good wife · *bhārgava* Śukra

**Meaning:** He is very happy, graceful and charming, has sons, wealth and his desires fulfilled, and has good friends and a good wife.

**Explanation:** Venus is the enemy of Jupiter, but in a dharmic sign its aspect yields domestic happiness rather than excess: wife, friends and children, all "good" (*su-*).

### Śloka 64 · Aspected by Śani

> प्रियवादिनं सुवाक्यं बहुश्रुतं सत्यवादिनं सौम्यम् । अभिजातं नृपपुरुषं जनयति सौरेक्षितः शशी धनुषि ॥६४॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.64)*
>
> *priyavādinaṃ suvākyaṃ bahuśrutaṃ satyavādinaṃ saumyam | abhijātaṃ nṛpapuruṣaṃ janayati saurekṣitaḥ śaśī dhanuṣi || 64 ||*

**Key words:** *priyavādin* speaking pleasantly · *suvākya* eloquent · *bahuśruta* widely learned · *satyavādin* truthful · *saumya* gentle · *abhijāta* well-born · *nṛpapuruṣa* king's officer · *saura* son of Sūrya (Śani)

**Meaning:** He speaks pleasantly and well, is widely learned, truthful, gentle and well-born, and becomes a royal officer.

**Explanation:** This is the most striking verse of the chapter: elsewhere Saturn brings lies (ślokas 7, 29, 36, 57), but in Guru's sign it brings truthfulness. Jupiter's dharma disciplines Saturn, turning severity into integrity and patient learning.

## 10. Moon in Makara (ślokas 66–71)

Makara is Śani's earthy sign of labour, and the results follow each aspecting graha's dignity here. Mars (exalted in Makara) gives great wealth. Jupiter gives kingship, because it aspects Makara from signs where it is strong. Mercury, Venus and Saturn bring restlessness, scandal or vice.

### Śloka 66 · Aspected by Sūrya

> अधनं दुःखितमटनं परकर्मरतं मलीमसं कुरुते । मकरे कुवलयनाथः शिल्पमतिं वीक्षितो रविणा ॥६६॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.66)*
>
> *adhanaṃ duḥkhitamaṭanaṃ parakarmarataṃ malīmasaṃ kurute | makare kuvalayanāthaḥ śilpamatiṃ vīkṣito raviṇā || 66 ||*

**Key words:** *adhana* poor · *duḥkhita* sorrowful · *aṭana* wandering · *parakarmarata* doing others' work · *malīmasa* unclean · *śilpamati* with a mind for crafts · *kuvalayanātha* lord of the night-lotuses (Moon)

**Meaning:** He is poor, sorrowful, a wanderer who works for others, untidy, but with a gift for crafts.

**Explanation:** The Sun is Saturn's enemy, and in Saturn's sign of labour its aspect puts the native in service to others. The craft-mind (*śilpamati*) is Makara's practical skill surviving under poor circumstances.

### Śloka 67 · Aspected by Maṅgala

> अतिविभवमत्युदारं सुभगं धनसंयुतं मृगे पुरुषम् । वाहनयुतं प्रचण्डं करोति वक्रेक्षितश्चन्द्रः ॥६७॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.67)*
>
> *ativibhavamatyudāraṃ subhagaṃ dhanasaṃyutaṃ mṛge puruṣam | vāhanayutaṃ pracaṇḍaṃ karoti vakrekṣitaścandraḥ || 67 ||*

**Key words:** *ativibhava* very prosperous · *atyudāra* very generous · *subhaga* fortunate · *dhanasaṃyuta* wealthy · *mṛga* the deer (Makara) · *vāhana* vehicles · *pracaṇḍa* fierce

**Meaning:** He is very prosperous, very generous, fortunate and wealthy, with vehicles, and fierce in temperament.

**Explanation:** Mars is exalted in Makara, so its aspect on the Moon there carries Mars at full strength: energy that produces wealth. The generosity (*atyudāra*) is the exalted Mars's confidence.

### Śloka 68 · Aspected by Budha

> मूर्खं प्रवासशीलं गतयुवतिं चञ्चलं मृगे तीक्ष्णम् । जनयति बुधेन दृष्टः सुखरहितं निर्धनं पुरुषम् ॥६८॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.68)*
>
> *mūrkhaṃ pravāsaśīlaṃ gatayuvatiṃ cañcalaṃ mṛge tīkṣṇam | janayati budhena dṛṣṭaḥ sukharahitaṃ nirdhanaṃ puruṣam || 68 ||*

**Key words:** *mūrkha* foolish · *pravāsaśīla* always living away from home · *gatayuvati* whose wife has left · *cañcala* fickle · *tīkṣṇa* harsh · *nirdhana* penniless

**Meaning:** He is foolish, always away from home, abandoned by his wife, fickle and harsh, unhappy and penniless.

**Explanation:** Budha is the Moon's enemy. In Saturn's heavy sign its restlessness has no direction, so it shows as constant travel, instability in marriage and poor judgement.

### Śloka 69 · Aspected by Guru

> भूपतिमनुपमवीर्यं नृपतिगुणैः संयुतं मृगे जातम् । बहुदारपुत्रमित्रं जनयति गुरुवीक्षितश्चन्द्रः ॥६९॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.69)*
>
> *bhūpatimanupamavīryaṃ nṛpatiguṇaiḥ saṃyutaṃ mṛge jātam | bahudāraputramitraṃ janayati guruvīkṣitaścandraḥ || 69 ||*

**Key words:** *bhūpati* king · *anupamavīrya* of matchless valour · *nṛpatiguṇa* royal qualities · *bahu-dāra-putra-mitra* many wives, sons and friends

**Meaning:** He becomes a king of matchless valour with royal qualities, and has many wives, sons and friends.

**Explanation:** Guru is debilitated in Makara, but here it is the aspecting planet, not the occupant. Jupiter can aspect Makara from Karka (its 7th aspect), where it is exalted, and from the earth signs Vṛṣabha and Kanyā; none of these are its debilitation, which explains the regal result.

### Śloka 70 · Aspected by Śukra

> परयुवतिधनविभूषणवाहनमालान्वितं नरं मकरे । सोपक्रोशमपुत्रं जनयति भृगुवीक्षितश्चन्द्रः ॥७०॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.70)*
>
> *parayuvatidhanavibhūṣaṇavāhanamālānvitaṃ naraṃ makare | sopakrośamaputraṃ janayati bhṛguvīkṣitaścandraḥ || 70 ||*

**Key words:** *parayuvati* other men's wives · *dhana* wealth · *vibhūṣaṇa* ornaments · *vāhana* vehicles · *mālā* garlands · *sopakrośa* subject to censure · *aputra* without sons

**Meaning:** He enjoys other men's wives, wealth, ornaments, vehicles and garlands, but is criticised and has no sons.

**Explanation:** Venus is Saturn's friend and gives comfort in Makara, but the pleasures are taken from others (*para-*). The price is public censure and a lack of sons.

### Śloka 71 · Aspected by Śani

> अलसं मलिनं सधनं मदनार्तं पारदारिकमसत्यम् । दिवसकरपुत्रदृष्टः करोति चन्द्रो नरं मकरे ॥७१॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.71)*
>
> *alasaṃ malinaṃ sadhanaṃ madanārtaṃ pāradārikamasatyam | divasakaraputradṛṣṭaḥ karoti candro naraṃ makare || 71 ||*

**Key words:** *alasa* lazy · *malina* unclean · *sadhana* wealthy · *madanārta* tormented by desire · *pāradārika* adulterous · *asatya* untruthful · *divasakaraputra* son of the day-maker (Śani)

**Meaning:** He is lazy and untidy, yet wealthy; tormented by desire, adulterous and untruthful.

**Explanation:** Saturn aspects the Moon in its own sign, so it protects wealth (*sadhana*), unlike most Saturn aspects. But Saturn's own vices dominate the mind: inertia, neglect and hidden desires.

## 11. Moon in Kumbha (ślokas 73–78)

Kumbha is Śani's airy sign, and here the benefics give modest results while two malefics give mixed ones. Jupiter alone gives a full, noble life. Venus, unusually, gives the worst result in this sign. Saturn, at home, keeps property intact but spoils character.

### Śloka 73 · Aspected by Sūrya

> अतिमलिनमति च शूरं नृपरूपं धार्मिकं कृषिकरं च । कुरुते दिनकरदृष्टो घटधरसंस्थः क्षपानाथः ॥७३॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.73)*
>
> *atimalinamati ca śūraṃ nṛparūpaṃ dhārmikaṃ kṛṣikaraṃ ca | kurute dinakaradṛṣṭo ghaṭadharasaṃsthaḥ kṣapānāthaḥ || 73 ||*

**Key words:** *atimalina* very unclean · *mati* mind · *śūra* brave · *nṛparūpa* with the looks of a king · *dhārmika* religious · *kṛṣikara* farmer · *ghaṭadhara* the water-bearer (Kumbha) · *kṣapānātha* lord of the night (Moon)

**Meaning:** He is careless or impure in mind, yet brave, kingly in appearance, religious, and a farmer.

**Explanation:** The Sun aspects from Siṃha, its own sign opposite Kumbha. The native carries a ruler's bearing and religious habit, but works the land. The words *atimalina mati* are hard to read in the print; I take them as "careless of purity" [?].

### Śloka 74 · Aspected by Maṅgala

> कुम्भेऽतिसत्यवाक्यं मातृगुरुधनैर्वियुक्तमलसं च । विषमं परकार्यरतं करोति भौमेक्षितश्चन्द्रः ॥७४॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.74)*
>
> *kumbhe'tisatyavākyaṃ mātṛgurudhanairviyuktamalasaṃ ca | viṣamaṃ parakāryarataṃ karoti bhaumekṣitaścandraḥ || 74 ||*

**Key words:** *atisatyavākya* very truthful · *mātṛ-guru-dhana* mother, elders, wealth · *viyukta* separated from · *alasa* lazy · *viṣama* difficult, uneven · *parakāryarata* busy with others' affairs · *bhauma* son of the Earth (Maṅgala)

**Meaning:** He is very truthful, but separated from his mother, elders and wealth. He is lazy, difficult to deal with, and busy with other people's affairs.

**Explanation:** Mars gives Kumbha's idealism a blunt honesty. On the Moon, it severs the native from home supports (mother, teachers, family money), and its energy goes into causes that belong to others.

### Śloka 75 · Aspected by Budha

> अशनोपचारकुशलं गीतविधिज्ञं प्रियं च युवतीनाम् । तनुविभवसुखं पुरुषं करोति बुधवीक्षितः शशी कुम्भे ॥७५॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.75)*
>
> *aśanopacārakuśalaṃ gītavidhijñaṃ priyaṃ ca yuvatīnām | tanuvibhavasukhaṃ puruṣaṃ karoti budhavīkṣitaḥ śaśī kumbhe || 75 ||*

**Key words:** *aśana* food · *upacāra* service, hospitality · *kuśala* skilled · *gītavidhijña* knowing music · *priya yuvatīnām* dear to women · *tanu* little · *vibhava-sukha* wealth and happiness

**Meaning:** He is skilled in serving food and hospitality, knows music, is dear to women, but has little wealth or happiness.

**Explanation:** Budha, a friend of Saturn, gives Kumbha's social sign the skills of a host: food, music, pleasant company. The gifts are social, not material, so the purse stays small (*tanu*).

### Śloka 76 · Aspected by Guru

> ग्रामक्षेत्रतरूणां वरभवनानां वराङ्गनानां च । कुरुते भोगिनमार्यं साधुं गुरुवीक्षितः शशी कुम्भे ॥७६॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.76)*
>
> *grāmakṣetratarūṇāṃ varabhavanānāṃ varāṅganānāṃ ca | kurute bhoginamāryaṃ sādhuṃ guruvīkṣitaḥ śaśī kumbhe || 76 ||*

**Key words:** *grāma* villages · *kṣetra* fields · *taru* trees, orchards · *varabhavana* fine houses · *varāṅganā* fine women · *bhogin* one who enjoys · *ārya* noble · *sādhu* virtuous

**Meaning:** He owns and enjoys villages, fields, orchards, fine houses and fine women, and is noble and virtuous.

**Explanation:** Guru's aspect turns Kumbha's collective, landed side (villages, estates) into ownership, and its dharma keeps the enjoyment noble (*ārya*, *sādhu*).

### Śloka 77 · Aspected by Śukra

> नीचमपुत्रममित्रं कातरमाचार्यनिन्दितं पापम् । कुरुते शशी कुयुवतिं सितेक्षितो घटधरेऽल्पसुखम् ॥७७॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.77)*
>
> *nīcamaputramamitraṃ kātaramācāryaninditaṃ pāpam | kurute śaśī kuyuvatiṃ sitekṣito ghaṭadhare'lpasukham || 77 ||*

**Key words:** *nīca* low · *aputra* sonless · *amitra* friendless · *kātara* timid · *ācāryanindita* censured by his teachers · *pāpa* sinful · *kuyuvati* with a bad wife · *alpasukha* of little happiness

**Meaning:** He is low, without sons or friends, timid, censured by his teachers, sinful, has a bad wife, and little happiness.

**Explanation:** Venus is Saturn's friend, yet this is the harshest Venus aspect in the chapter. In Kumbha, Venus aspects from Siṃha (opposite, the Sun's sign) or from trines; the text does not give its reasoning, so take this as Sārāvalī's observation rather than a rule derived from friendship.

### Śloka 78 · Aspected by Śani

> नखरोमधरं मलिनं परदाररतं शठं विधर्माणम् । स्थावरभागिनमाढ्यं शशी घटे सौरसंदृष्टः ॥७८॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.78)*
>
> *nakharomadharaṃ malinaṃ paradārarataṃ śaṭhaṃ vidharmāṇam | sthāvarabhāginamāḍhyaṃ śaśī ghaṭe saurasaṃdṛṣṭaḥ || 78 ||*

**Key words:** *nakha-roma-dhara* with long nails and body hair · *malina* unclean · *paradārarata* adulterous · *śaṭha* deceitful · *vidharman* irreligious · *sthāvara* immovable property · *āḍhya* rich · *ghaṭa* the pot (Kumbha)

**Meaning:** He keeps long nails and hair, is unclean, adulterous, deceitful and irreligious, yet rich and owns land and buildings.

**Explanation:** As in Makara (śloka 71), Saturn aspecting the Moon in its own sign protects possessions, here landed property (*sthāvara*). The cost is the same: Saturn's neglect of the body and of conduct.

## 12. Moon in Mīna (ślokas 80–85)

Mīna is Guru's watery sign and Śukra's exaltation. Five of the six aspects give comfort, rank or charm. Mars and Saturn are the exceptions, falling hardest on the native's family and his self-respect.

### Śloka 80 · Aspected by Sūrya

> तीव्रमदनप्रकाशं सुखिनं सेनापतिं धनसमृद्धम् । जनयति दिनकरदृष्टः सुमुदितभार्यं शशी मीने ॥८०॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.80)*
>
> *tīvramadanaprakāśaṃ sukhinaṃ senāpatiṃ dhanasamṛddham | janayati dinakaradṛṣṭaḥ sumuditabhāryaṃ śaśī mīne || 80 ||*

**Key words:** *tīvramadana* intense desire · *prakāśa* showing openly · *sukhin* happy · *senāpati* army commander · *dhanasamṛddha* wealthy · *sumuditabhārya* whose wife is content

**Meaning:** He is openly passionate, happy, an army commander, wealthy, and his wife is content.

**Explanation:** The Sun, Guru's friend, gives command in Guru's sign. Pisces's sensual water and the Sun's heat show as strong desire, but here it is directed into a happy marriage.

### Śloka 81 · Aspected by Maṅgala

> परिभूतं सुखरहितं कुलटापुत्रं च पापनिरतं च । जनयति नक्षत्रपतिः क्षितिसुतदृष्टो झषे शूरम् ॥८१॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.81)*
>
> *paribhūtaṃ sukharahitaṃ kulaṭāputraṃ ca pāpanirataṃ ca | janayati nakṣatrapatiḥ kṣitisutadṛṣṭo jhaṣe śūram || 81 ||*

**Key words:** *paribhūta* humiliated · *sukharahita* unhappy · *kulaṭāputra* son of an unchaste woman · *pāpanirata* given to wrongdoing · *śūra* brave · *jhaṣa* the fish (Mīna) · *nakṣatrapati* lord of the stars (Moon)

**Meaning:** He is humiliated and unhappy, is born of an unchaste mother, is given to wrongdoing, but is brave.

**Explanation:** Mars on the Moon in a soft water sign has no outlet except conflict and scandal. The slur on the mother is Mars striking the Moon's first signification (the mother); courage is all that Mars leaves intact.

### Śloka 82 · Aspected by Budha

> जनयति बुधेन दृष्टो मीनस्थश्चन्द्रमाः पुरुषम् । भूपतिमतीव सुखिनं वरयुवतिसमावृतं वश्यम् ॥८२॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.82)*
>
> *janayati budhena dṛṣṭo mīnasthaścandramāḥ puruṣam | bhūpatimatīva sukhinaṃ varayuvatisamāvṛtaṃ vaśyam || 82 ||*

**Key words:** *bhūpati* king · *atīva sukhin* very happy · *varayuvati* fine women · *samāvṛta* surrounded by · *vaśya* under their sway

**Meaning:** He becomes a king, very happy, surrounded by fine women and under their sway.

**Explanation:** Budha aspects from Kanyā, its exaltation sign opposite Mīna. The exalted Mercury raises the Moon to kingship, while Pisces's softness leaves the native ruled by those around him.

### Śloka 83 · Aspected by Guru

> गुरुदृष्टो मीनस्थो ललितं चन्द्रोऽग्रमाण्डलिकम् । अत्याढ्यं सुकुमारं बहुभिः स्त्रीभिर्वृतं जनयेत् ॥८३॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.83)*
>
> *gurudṛṣṭo mīnastho lalitaṃ candro'gramāṇḍalikam | atyāḍhyaṃ sukumāraṃ bahubhiḥ strībhirvṛtaṃ janayet || 83 ||*

**Key words:** *lalita* graceful · *agramāṇḍalika* foremost regional ruler · *atyāḍhya* very rich · *sukumāra* delicate, refined · *strībhiḥ vṛta* surrounded by women

**Meaning:** He is graceful, the foremost of regional rulers, very rich, refined, and surrounded by many women.

**Explanation:** Guru aspects the Moon in its own sign, so the native gains rank and great wealth. The "delicate" quality (*sukumāra*) is Pisces's gentleness, not weakness.

### Śloka 84 · Aspected by Śukra

> कुरुते शशी सुशीलं रतिमन्तं नृत्यवाद्यगेयरतम् । शुक्रेक्षितो झषस्थो हृदयहरं कामिनीनां च ॥८४॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.84)*
>
> *kurute śaśī suśīlaṃ ratimantaṃ nṛtyavādyageyaratam | śukrekṣito jhaṣastho hṛdayaharaṃ kāminīnāṃ ca || 84 ||*

**Key words:** *suśīla* of good character · *ratimant* skilled in love · *nṛtya-vādya-geya* dance, instrumental music, song · *rata* devoted to · *hṛdayahara* heart-stealing · *kāminī* loving women

**Meaning:** He is of good character, skilled in love, devoted to dance, music and song, and steals the hearts of women.

**Explanation:** Śukra is exalted in Mīna, so it supplies the full set of the three arts (*nṛtya, vādya, gīta*). Note *suśīla* (good character): exalted Venus refines desire rather than coarsening it.

### Śloka 85 · Aspected by Śani

> विकलमहितं जनन्याः कामार्तं पुत्रदारमतिहीनम् । कुरुते रविसुतदृष्टो नीचविरूपाङ्गनासक्तम् ॥८५॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.85)*
>
> *vikalamahitaṃ jananyāḥ kāmārtaṃ putradāramatihīnam | kurute ravisutadṛṣṭo nīcavirūpāṅganāsaktam || 85 ||*

**Key words:** *vikala* impaired · *ahitaṃ jananyāḥ* harmful to the mother · *kāmārta* tormented by desire · *putra-dāra-mati-hīna* without sons, wife or good sense · *nīca-virūpa-aṅganā* low and ugly women · *āsakta* attached

**Meaning:** He is impaired in body, harmful to his mother, tormented by desire, without sons, wife or good sense, and attached to low, ugly women.

**Explanation:** Saturn on the Moon in a watery, sensual sign leaves desire without its satisfactions: the opposite of Śukra's result in śloka 84. The mother suffers here too, as with most Saturn aspects in this chapter.

## 13. Closing rule (śloka 86) and the chapter at a glance

Śloka 86 tells you when these results apply: only in full when the sign lord, the sign and the Moon are all strong. Otherwise each result is scaled by dignity.

> राशिपतौ बलयुक्ते राशौ च बलान्विते तथा चन्द्रे । राशिफलं स्यात् सकलं नीचोच्चविधिना च संचिन्त्यम् ॥८६॥ *(Sārāvalī 23.86)*
>
> *rāśipatau balayukte rāśau ca balānvite tathā candre | rāśiphalaṃ syāt sakalaṃ nīcoccavidhinā ca saṃcintyam || 86 ||*

**Key words:** *rāśipati* lord of the sign · *balayukta* strong · *rāśiphala* the result of the sign · *sakala* in full · *nīcocca-vidhi* the rule of debilitation and exaltation · *saṃcintya* to be weighed

**Meaning:** When the lord of the sign is strong, the sign is strong, and the Moon is strong, the result of the sign comes in full. The rules of debilitation and exaltation must also be weighed.

**Explanation:** This is the key to using the whole chapter. Every result above assumes a strong Moon, a strong dispositor and a strong sign. A weak dispositor (for example Maṅgala debilitated when the Moon is in Meṣa) spoils the good results; the exaltation or debilitation of the aspecting planet raises or lowers its result. Several verses show this rule at work (ślokas 7, 45, 50, 53, 67, 69, 84).

| Moon in | Sūrya | Maṅgala | Budha | Guru | Śukra | Śani |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Kanyā | Treasurer; no wife | Master craftsman | Astrologer, debater | King's work, happy | Many wives, rich | Poor; lives on wife's money |
| Tulā | Poor, sick, humiliated | Thief, adulterer | Artist, rich, famous | Honoured trader | Graceful, healthy, rich | Rich; good for mother |

| Dhanu | King | Army commander | Dramatist, astrologer | Minister | Happy family | Truthful royal officer |
| Makara | Poor servant; crafts | Very rich, generous | Restless, poor | King | Pleasures; censured | Rich but lazy, adulterous |
| Kumbha | Kingly-looking farmer | Truthful; cut off from family | Host, musician; small means | Estates; noble | Low, friendless | Landed; deceitful |

**Patterns across the chapter:**

- **Guru never gives a bad result.** Even in Vṛścika it gives wealth; in five signs it gives kingship or ministry.
- **Śani is harsh almost everywhere**, except in Tulā (exalted), Dhanu (Guru's sign) and its own two signs, where it at least protects property.
- **The Moon's mother suffers under Maṅgala and Śani** (ślokas 10, 14, 25, 29, 39, 43, 85), the two malefics striking the Moon's first signification.
- **Dhanu is the only sign where all six aspects are favourable.**
